Objective

The course aims to develop students’ foundational understanding of data analytics and their ability to transform data into
meaningful visual insights. It prepares students to recognize the value of data in business and society, understand basic
concepts in big data, artificial intelligence, and machine learning, and apply effective visualization principles in designing
charts, dashboards, and data-driven narratives that support informed decision-making.
